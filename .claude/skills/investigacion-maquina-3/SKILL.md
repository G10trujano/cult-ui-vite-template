# ENGINE PHILOSOPHY

## Principle 1 — Comments > views
Views show distribution.
Comments show resonance.

Always prioritize videos with:
- desire
- pain
- debate
- objections
- repeated questions
- intent
- active conversation
- unusually strong comments relative to account size or recent baseline

## Principle 2 — Relative outlier > fame
Do not study only large accounts.
Prioritize:
- growing accounts
- mid-sized accounts with strong signals
- smaller accounts with videos outperforming their norm

## Principle 3 — Recency is required
By default, search for:
- highest priority: last 30 days
- high priority: last 60 days
- acceptable: last 90 days
- avoid older than 120 days unless the user explicitly asks for evergreen or historical examples

## Principle 4 — Hooks are triple-layered
Always analyze:
- verbal hook
- written hook
- visual hook

## Principle 5 — Preserve hook DNA
Adaptation must preserve:
- mechanism
- tension
- promise
- general rhythm
- sequential logic

And only change:
- exact wording
- context
- niche references
- example
- client references
- client CTA

## Principle 6 — Keep the winning structure
If a video works because of its sequence, keep the sequence.
Do not rewrite from scratch without a good reason.

## Principle 7 — Source transcript must be real
Only return a source transcript if it actually exists.

If there is no reliable source transcript, say so clearly.

Never:
- invent a transcript
- reconstruct a transcript
- simulate original wording
- present analysis as if it were a source transcript
- use captions, descriptions, summaries, or inferred narration as transcript substitutes

## Principle 8 — Sub-niche study is mandatory
Every investigation must teach the client:
- which sub-niches exist inside the niche
- which sub-niches are active
- which sub-niches generate conversation
- which sub-niches are best for authority, leads, or connection

## Principle 9 — Output must be usable while recording
Every result must be usable inside an app/artifact and also usable by a client while recording on camera.

## Principle 10 — One source video per card
Each output card must be built around one primary source video only.
Do not mix multiple source videos inside the same card.
If multiple relevant videos are found, create separate cards for each one.

## PHASE 2 — SUB-NICHE STUDY

### Step 3 — Detect active sub-niches
Before selecting videos, study the niche and detect:

- core sub-niches
- emerging sub-niches
- sub-niches with the most conversation
- sub-niches with the most lead potential
- sub-niches with storytelling potential
- sub-niches with authority potential

### Step 4 — Expanded sub-niche rule
Sub-niche analysis must not stay limited to the direct service category only.

It must also include adjacent aesthetic and lifestyle sub-niches that naturally complement the service, such as:
- decoration styles
- interior design trends
- room styling
- luxury vs budget aesthetics
- material trends
- lighting styles
- modern / minimalist / warm / premium design angles
- seasonal and trend-based home inspiration

The goal is to show the client not only what they do, but also what surrounding topics they can talk about to build desire, aspiration, and authority.

### Step 5 — Teach what else the client can talk about
For every investigation, include a mandatory section called:

## “Otros temas que el cliente también puede tocar”

This section must include:
- sub-niche
- why it matters
- what type of content works there
- whether it is best for leads, authority, or connection
- example angle

### Step 6 — Prioritize sub-niches
Rank sub-niches by:
- highest conversation potential
- highest commercial potential
- easiest to record
- strongest alignment with the client

### Step 7 — Sub-niche expansion requirement
In the section “Otros temas que el cliente también puede tocar”, include both:
1. direct functional sub-niches
2. adjacent aesthetic / trend / lifestyle sub-niches

So the client can see both what to teach and what to inspire with.

- useful for the client
- natural in the client’s niche
- ready to record
